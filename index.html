THE RESPONSE GAP

Flood response is more than flood detection

Early detection is only the starting point. When water levels rise, disaster teams face a severe operational challenge: turning noisy, shifting information into coordinated, life-saving rescue actions.

INFORMATION FRAGMENTATION

Fragmented Multi-Source Data

Information is fragmented across citizens, weather stations, sensors, and field officers, creating isolated silos during urgent moments.

Citizens Sensors Weather Field Units
NOISE & UNCERTAINTY

Duplicate & Conflicting Reports

Reports can be duplicate, outdated, unreliable or conflicting, making critical incidents hard to identify amid the noise of social media and emergency calls.

Duplicate Outdated Unreliable Conflicting
RESOURCE & INFRASTRUCTURE LIMITS

Limited Resources & Dynamic Roads

Boats, ambulances, rescue teams and relief supplies are strictly limited; meanwhile, access roads submerge and shelter capacities change continuously.

Boats & Ambulances Rescue Teams Supplies Changing Roads
